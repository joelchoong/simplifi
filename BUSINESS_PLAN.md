# SimpliFi — Business & Product Roadmap

## 1. Executive Summary

**Product:**
A financial identity platform helping users in Malaysia understand, track, and benchmark their financial life over time.

**Core evolution:**
*   **Today:** “Money Health” (diagnostic tools)
*   **Next:** “Financial Memory” (tracking over time)
*   **Future:** “Financial Identity” (benchmark + positioning)

---

## 2. MVP Phase 1 — Current Product (Already Built)

We are not just an early-stage idea; we are already in **Phase 1: Financial Awareness Layer**.

### Existing Modules (Reframed):
*   **A. Income Classification (B40 / M40 / T20):** Tells users where they stand in Malaysia.
    *   👉 *Psychological hook:* “Am I behind or ahead?”
*   **B. Retirement (EPF Simulation):** Projects future savings based on contributions.
    *   👉 *Answers:* “Will I be okay long-term?”
*   **C. Income Reality (Expenses vs Income):** Shows actual usable income after lifestyle.
    *   👉 *Answers:* “Why do I feel broke?”
*   **D. Benchmarking (Malaysia + Global):** Compare salary locally and internationally.
    *   👉 *Answers:* “Am I underpaid?” | “What if I move?”

### 🔑 What Phase 1 really is:
👉 **A Diagnostic Engine**
*   Snapshot of financial health
*   High shareability
*   Great for content funnel
*   *Note:* It is currently "stateless" (no memory/history).

---

## 3. Phase 2 — Financial Memory Layer (The Next Move)

This phase transforms the product from a one-time usage tool into a long-term retention system.

*   **A. Tax Record System:** Store yearly tax (Income, Reliefs, Tax paid, Effective rate).
    *   👉 *Becomes:* “My tax history, not just a calculation.”
*   **B. Net Worth Tracker:** Monthly / quarterly snapshots with trend + milestones.
    *   👉 *Becomes:* “Am I progressing financially?”

---

## 4. Phase 3 — Insight + Progress Engine

Once users have history, the product begins to teach:
*   “Your net worth grew RM8,200 this quarter”
*   “Your savings rate improved vs last year”
*   “Your tax rate increased YoY”
*   👉 **Outcome:** Users don't just input data; they gain wisdom about their own behavior.

---

## 5. Phase 4 — Benchmarking 2.0 (The Unfair Advantage)

Leveraging real user data to evolve static benchmarks into dynamic positioning:
*   **Net worth percentile:** Comparison based on real data, not just static assumptions.
*   **Savings rate comparison:** How efficient are you compared to your peers?
*   **Tax efficiency comparison:** Are you missing out on reliefs others in your bracket are using?
*   👉 **Outcome:** This becomes the definitive "Where do I REALLY stand?" engine.

---

## 6. Product Architecture Shift

*   **Current:** Input → Output → Done
*   **Future:** Input → **Store** → Compare → Trend → Insight

*The “Store” step is the key to scalability and moat-building.*

---

## 7. Business Model

*   **Phase 1 (Growth):** Free tools to drive acquisition via content.
*   **Phase 2+ (Retention & Monetization):**
    *   **Free:** Basic tracking (limited history) and core diagnostics.
    *   **Paid/Premium:** Full history (multi-year tax, net worth), deep benchmarking insights, advanced projections, and “Financial Report” exports.

---

## 8. Go-To-Market Strategy

*   **Phase 1 Content:** “RM5k salary is actually RM3.8k” | “Are you B40, M40 or T20?”
*   **Phase 2 Content:** “I tracked my net worth for 12 months — here’s what happened” | “Most Malaysians don’t know their past tax numbers.”
*   **Phase 3 Content:** “If your net worth is below this, you’re falling behind” | “Top 20% savings rate in Malaysia looks like this.”

---

## 9. Key Metrics

*   **Phase 1:** Tool completion rate, Share rate.
*   **Phase 2:** % users logging monthly net worth, % users returning after 30 days.
*   **Phase 3:** Insight engagement, Benchmark interaction rate.

---

## 10. Strategic Positioning

We are **NOT** building:
*   ❌ A budgeting app
*   ❌ A robo-advisor
*   ❌ A generic finance calculator site

We **ARE** building:
*   ✅ **“The system that tells you where you stand financially — over time.”**

---

## 11. The Brutal Insight

*   **Current Product:** High reach, Low retention.
*   **Phase 2 (Tax + Net Worth):** Lower reach, High retention.
*   **Strategy:** We need both to win. Reach builds the funnel; Memory builds the business.

---

## 12. Next Steps (Concrete Plan)

1.  **Step 1:** Implement a **Net Worth Tracker** (Monthly, dead simple).
2.  **Step 2:** Implement **Tax History** (Yearly input).
3.  **Step 3:** Deliver the **First Insight**: Show simple "Up/Down" trends vs the last period.
